# How to Manage Your Consultation Locations on Doctoralia

This article describes how to manage your physical and online consultation locations, update address details, change the order in which locations appear on your profile, and resolve common issues.

## Viewing your consultation locations

You can view all your physical and online consultation locations.
To view your consultation locations:
1. Sign in to your Doctoralia account on the website.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Select **Addresses**.

From this section, you can manage the consultation locations associated with your professional profile.

> [!NOTE]
> Can I manage addresses from the mobile app?
> Adding, editing, deleting, and reordering consultation locations are available through the Doctoralia website, not the mobile app. Use a web browser to make these changes.

## Adding a consultation location

You can add physical consultation locations or an online consultation location to your professional profile, subject to your subscription plan.

### Adding a physical consultation location

To add a physical consultation location:
1. On the Doctoralia website, go to **Settings** > **Addresses**.
2. Select **Add physical consultation**.
3. Enter the required address details:
   - Street name
   - Street number
   - Postal code
4. If needed, provide additional details such as the floor, apartment, building, or entrance.
5. Complete the remaining fields shown on the form and save your new consultation location.

Additional address details can help patients find the correct entrance or office.

> [!NOTE]
> How many physical consultation locations can I add?
> The number of physical consultation locations available to your account depends on your subscription plan. If you cannot add another location, see the troubleshooting section below.

### Adding an online consultation location

To add an online consultation location:
1. On the Doctoralia website, go to **Settings** > **Addresses**.
2. Select **Add online consultation**.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions to create your online consultation location.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Paid plans allow one online consultation location per professional.

If the **Add online consultation** button is missing, check whether you already have an online consultation location registered.

## Editing an existing location

You can update the details of an existing consultation location whenever you need to correct or change its address information.

To edit an existing location:
1. On the Doctoralia website, go to **Settings** > **Addresses**.
2. Find the card for the consultation location you want to update.
3. Select **Edit address**.
4. Update the relevant address fields.
5. Select **Save changes**.

### Updating additional location details

To update floor, apartment, or entrance details:
1. Open **Edit address** for the relevant consultation location.
2. Find **Additional information (optional)**.
3. Enter the updated floor, apartment, or entrance details.
4. Select **Save changes**.

## Reordering your locations

You can change the order of your consultation locations from the **Addresses** section:
1. On the Doctoralia website, go to **Settings** > **Addresses**.
2. Find the consultation location you want to move.
3. Hold the drag handle on the left side of its card.
4. Drag the card to your preferred position.
5. Check for the **Saved successfully** confirmation.

The consultation location in the first position is considered your primary address.

> [!NOTE]
> Why hasn't the order changed on my public profile?
> Changes to your public profile may take some time to appear. In addition, the patient-facing profile may group or prioritize locations according to consultation type. If the new order is not visible immediately, wait a little and check your public profile in a private or incognito browser window.

## Deleting a consultation location

Before removing a consultation location, check whether one of the following restrictions applies:
- The location is the only address registered on your profile.
- The location has a linked calendar.

An address cannot be deleted while either restriction applies.

> [!NOTE]
> What if the location has a linked calendar?
> If a calendar is linked to the address, you need to delete the calendar first before you delete the address.
 
### Deleting a calendar

Deleting a calendar has important consequences for scheduled appointments and the associated configuration.

In particular, deleting a calendar:
- Cancels future appointments without notifying affected patients.
- Removes the associated services and consultation hours.
- Cannot be undone through the normal workflow. If deleted accidentally, contact the support team to ask whether they can restore the calendar.
- Preserves the history of past appointments.

If you decide to proceed with calendar deletion after reviewing the consequences:
1. On the Doctoralia website, go to **Settings** > **Calendars**.
2. Select the calendar linked to the consultation location.
3. Open the options menu in the upper-right corner.
4. Select **Delete calendar** only after confirming that you understand the consequences.

You can then return to **Settings** > **Addresses** to continue removing the consultation location, provided no other restriction applies.

If you have upcoming appointments, review them before taking any action that could affect the calendar.

## Troubleshooting common issues

### I can't find the button to add a physical consultation location

If **Add physical consultation** is missing:
1. Check that you are using the Doctoralia website.
2. Open **Settings** > **Addresses**.
3. Look for a message such as **You cannot add more addresses**.

Your subscription plan may limit the number of physical consultation locations you can register.

If you are unsure of your account's limit, contact Customer Care for clarification.

### I can't find the option to add an online consultation

If **Add online consultation** is missing:
1. Check whether an online consultation location is already registered on your profile.
2. Look for a general Add control that may provide access to the same action.
3. If you have a paid plan, remember that the documented allowance is one online consultation location per professional.

If the option remains unavailable, contact the support team to clarify your account's eligibility.

### Patients cannot book appointments at my new consultation location

If your patients cannot book appointments at your new consultation location, check that both of the following are configured:
- At least one service is assigned to your consultation location.
- Consultation hours are configured for your consultation location in its calendar.

If either is missing, configure it before expecting patients to be able to book appointments at that location.

### My public profile still shows the old location order

If you have already reordered your consultation locations:
1. Check whether the **Saved successfully** message appeared right after reordering your consultation locations.
2. Allow time for the public profile to update.
3. Open your public profile in a private or incognito browser window.
4. Check the location order, keeping in mind that locations may be grouped by consultation type.

## When to contact support

Some consultation location issues require assistance from the Doctoralia support team.

### Adding another consultation location

Contact Customer Care if you cannot add another consultation location and need to understand the location limits associated with your account.

The team can review your account-specific situation and provide guidance.

### Recovering an accidentally deleted location

If you have deleted a consultation location by mistake, contact the support team to request assistance.

You cannot restore deleted addresses yourself. The support team may be able to restore the deleted address and calendar, but recovery should not be assumed to restore associated appointments or configuration.

### Other unresolved problems

Contact the support team if you have followed the relevant steps but still cannot add, edit, reorder, or remove a consultation location.

When describing the issue, explain what you were trying to do, which section of the website you were using, and any message displayed on the screen.
