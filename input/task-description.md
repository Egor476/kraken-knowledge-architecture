# Instructions
Kraken is the AI assistant that helps Doctoralia doctors manage their profile. The video linked
below shows a conversation between a doctor in Mexico and Kraken about her addresses on
Doctoralia. The conversation is in Spanish.

Watch the video. The conversation allows you to see the product instructions, the tone of voice,
and the cases where Kraken hands the request over to a specialist (a Customer Care agent).
Based on what you see, create:
1. Kraken instructions: the content Kraken uses to answer doctors on this topic. Imagine
that the conversation in the video is the result you want to get, and start from zero.
First, list the product facts you would expect to find in the product code, for example
rules, limits, buttons and error messages. Then turn them into the instructions for
Kraken, including tone of voice, language, escalations and product steps.
Write the instructions so that Kraken can pick only the parts it needs for each question.
The same parts will be reused for other markets and plans, and when the product
changes, we want to update only the part that changed.
2. Knowledge base article for customers: the article doctors read in the help center.
Imagine that Kraken can also send the doctor to this article, so the doctor can read it on
their own.
3. Knowledge base article for internal stakeholders: the article our internal teams use,
for example Customer Care and Customer Success. Think about what extra information
these teams need. You can invent these details.

You choose the format of the output. You can write in English or Spanish.

Amadeo will schedule a 45-minute call with Francesca Agostini and Felipe Diaz. In the call you
present your work and we discuss it together.
